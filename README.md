## About Me

I build end-to-end ML systems — from data pipelines and model fine-tuning through to production deployment. My work sits at the intersection of **NLP, LLMs, and applied computer vision**, with hands-on experience shipping RAG pipelines, BERT-based re-rankers, and geospatial AI applications.

Currently at **ASU's Decision Theater**, I develop LLM-powered natural-language interfaces over census and geospatial datasets, and optimize inference on Intel Gaudi HPUs and NVIDIA GPUs. Previously at **DigitalMain**, I fine-tuned cross-encoder models for search re-ranking (93% accuracy) and designed prompt pipelines for RAG, summarization, and Q&A automation across 5+ LLM APIs.
